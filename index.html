How it works

Four lanes. One autonomous loop.

Every incident runs the same closed loop a good SRE runs — except it runs on every alert, holds the whole history in view, and never has to context-switch away halfway through.

  1. 01

    Observe

    Streams metrics, logs, traces, deploys and change events from every cluster and cloud. Learns each service's normal — per region, per hour, per release.

    • Adaptive baselines
    • Deploy & config correlation
    • Noise suppression
  2. 02

    Diagnose

    Forms hypotheses, queries your telemetry to test them, and returns a ranked root cause with the evidence chain — not a wall of dashboards.

    • Causal graph over services
    • Evidence-linked reasoning
    • Confidence scoring
  3. 03

    Act

    Executes the remediation inside policies you write — scale, failover, roll back, drain, patch — or stops and asks when the blast radius crosses your line.

    • Policy-as-code guardrails
    • Dry-run & approval modes
    • Automatic rollback
  4. 04

    Learn

    Writes the postmortem, opens the fix PR, updates the runbook, and remembers the pattern so the next occurrence is caught in seconds.

    • Auto-drafted postmortems
    • Runbook synthesis
    • Reliability memory
Capabilities

An SRE that actually operates your stack

Not a summarizer bolted onto your alerts. Tetralane holds live state of your infrastructure and takes accountable action inside it.

Anomaly detection that fits your traffic

Seasonality-aware baselines per service and region catch the slow burns static thresholds miss — and stay quiet during your Black Friday peak.

Root cause with a paper trail

Every conclusion links to the query, span and diff that produced it. Reviewable reasoning, so your senior engineers can check the conclusion instead of taking it on faith.

Guardrails you write in code

Declare what the agent may touch, when, and how far. Blast-radius limits, change windows, and mandatory human approval per environment.

Remediation in seconds, not sprints

Scale, restart, reroute, roll back or failover across Kubernetes, serverless and VMs — verified against SLOs before the incident is closed.

Postmortems written for humans

Timeline, impact, contributing factors and action items drafted the moment an incident closes — in your template, in your incident tool.

SLO & error-budget aware

Prioritises by budget burn, not alert volume. Tetralane knows which fire actually threatens the quarter's reliability targets.
